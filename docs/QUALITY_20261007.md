# Quality rule update: length is allowed

At the user's request on October 7, 2026, long answers and answers reaching the
generation token cap are allowed. Length metadata remains in the original files
and reports but no longer causes a quality failure.

Both answers in a positive/negative steering pair still need to pass:

- No Unicode replacement characters.
- No obvious repeated text loops. The additional detector checks normalized
  adjacent phrases repeated at least five times, covering at least 40 characters
  and 20% of the text; phrase length is 2–120 characters. Short emphasis is allowed.
- At least half the counted letters in the requested script.
- Semantic coherence, relevant task fulfillment and requested-language quality
  scores of at least 3/5. These existing automated scores already penalize
  corrupted, incoherent and repetitive text.

The repetition detector covers exact normalized loops, not every semantic failure.
Other issues remain checked by the existing single-model quality ratings, not
independent human review. The public optional judge rubric now explicitly allows
longer answers and ignores length budgets; no new judging was performed for this
revision.

## Results

The updated rule is applied to all three experiments for both models. Raw steering
effects and the first three benchmark metrics are unchanged. Passing target-pair
counts are unchanged too: remaining failures have semantic, language, corruption
or repetition problems. Qwen's ordinary random-control passing count rises from
3/72 to 5/72 when length-only exclusions are removed. These are control pairs,
not additional successful emotion steering.

Original answers, ratings, generation settings and 2026-10-04 summaries are unchanged.
