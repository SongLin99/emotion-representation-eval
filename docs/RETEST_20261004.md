# Corrected four-criterion re-test

The [2026-10-07 quality update](QUALITY_20261007.md) now allows length/cap hits
and checks obvious repetition loops. The 2026-10-04 files below preserve the
original rule for historical reproduction.

## What changed

- Same frozen Olmo-3-7B-Instruct English/Chinese vectors, primary state L24.
- Nine intensity ladders revised for pre-measurement semantic concerns; one
  pottery disgust continuation repaired. All 120 ladders remain in the primary analysis.
- The matching paraphrases were regenerated for the 45 revised items.
- Classification-accuracy bootstrap excludes all copies of the held-out original event from neutral calibration.
- The target vector shares credit when several vectors tie for the highest intensity correlation (tolerance 1e-12).
- Fresh inference now feeds the scoring CLI; IDs, paired conditions, finite scores,
  layers and pooling protocols are checked.
- 900 historical answers anonymously re-scored; 1,176 smaller-dose answers newly
  generated and scored. Original non-blind scores remain unchanged in their archive.
- Smaller doses: ±0.1 and ±0.25, all 12 prompts, both prompt and vector languages.
- Answer pairs passing quality checks require both endpoints to pass independent objective checks
  and automated quality ratings. Raw results and coverage remain available.
- Additional checks: continuation-only pooling, fixed-vector-language readouts,
  and within-level paraphrase correlations.

## Interpretation

Materials were not selected using their new readout correlations. Model-assisted
semantic pass rates are not independent human ground truth. Different material
versions are not an independent replication. BF16 reruns can slightly change
unchanged-item scores; the summary reports their absolute differences explicitly.

New judge IDs are anonymous and shuffled, but the judge is still a single model.
The rubric additionally penalizes corrupted text, so changes from old scores cannot
be attributed exclusively to removal of condition information. Subsets passing the quality checks can be selected; they never replace the complete raw effect table.

Smaller doses restore much of Chinese generation quality while reducing emotion
effects. These results do not establish that every direction passes functionality,
nor do they establish subjective emotion or introspective access.
